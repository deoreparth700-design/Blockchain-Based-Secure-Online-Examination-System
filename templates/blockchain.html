🔒 Tamper-Evident Architecture

Upon submission, student marks and timestamps are sealed into a block containing the SHA-256 hash pointer of the immediately preceding block. Any subsequent attempt to alter student scores or answers breaks the cryptographic sequence, making the tampering immediately detectable during validation.
