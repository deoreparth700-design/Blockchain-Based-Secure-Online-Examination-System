How Cryptographic Tamper-Detection Works:

Each block seals the candidate's name, roll number, and exam score together with the Previous Block's Hash into a unique 256-bit SHA-256 fingerprint. If any adversary modifies a student's score in the database, recomputing the hash immediately produces a completely different value, catching the unauthorized change instantly.
